Case Study • 01

Sample Lead & Contact Tracking Project — Home Cleaning Service

View Live Google Sheet (opens in a new tab)
Problem
A small home cleaning service had 50 customer inquiries recorded in a basic spreadsheet. Customer names, contact details, requested services, locations, and inquiry statuses were entered inconsistently, making it difficult to quickly identify new leads and follow up with customers who had not yet received a response.
Solution
I organized the 50 records in Google Sheets using fields for customer details, service, location, inquiry date, status, and follow-up date. I standardized the data and added New, Contacted, Follow-Up, Booked, and Closed status categories with filters and formatting.
Result
The 50 customer inquiries were turned into one organized lead tracker that made it easier to identify new inquiries, monitor follow-ups, and see which customers had already been contacted or booked. The spreadsheet provided a clearer, more manageable system for tracking customer inquiries.

Note: This is a sample project created to demonstrate my administrative skills, not paid client work.
